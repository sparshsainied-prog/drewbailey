Volume V of The Giftborn Chronicles

Nothing But Ghosts Now

House Lanier thought destroying the nether would be their salvation, risking everything to vanquish the ravenous creature from their suffering world. But when it comes to the rules of sorcery, one really must be mindful of the spells they invoke.

Now the survivors of the godsgate ritual find themselves in unfamiliar territory, where the ocean rolls off far into the horizon and the heavens wear a strange shape of stars. Something fresh has caught the scent of them. Something cold, calculating, and undeniable. Something that will stop at nothing to see its patience and perseverance rewarded, even if it means remaking civilisation from the ground up.

The story so far

  1. Volume I

    The Royal Nothings

    Tired of being a puppet prince, Remy turned in the crown for a watchman's sword. Tired of being a protected princess, Marsea turned in her petticoats for an assassin's cloak. And Aiden, the best blood-mage of his class, turns in his laurels for another bottle of the black.

  2. Volume II

    They Once Had Names

    One lays dying, one just lost their beloved, and the third is lost amidst rogues and wretches, as the blight descends on Lancastle and House Lanier.

  3. Volume III

    Even the Favored Suffer

    A new age dawns, and with it prophecy has come for the royal nothings, with the aid of a primordial being called the gift.

  4. Volume IV

    Until the Crows Delight

    A desperate race to unearth a storied incantation that may be the only means of saving the moon of Vaelsyntheria from a legion of eldritch creatures.

  5. Volume V

    Nothing But Ghosts Now

    The survivors of the godsgate ritual wake under a strange shape of stars. Out now.

About Drew

Drew Bailey writes horror and fantasy. He went to college to expand his knowledge of literature and history, and it still took him the better part of a decade to mould it into something worth chasing after. Better late than never, as they say.

The Giftborn Chronicles is his first published series. In his spare time he is a chronic coffee drinker, an avid horror movie watcher, and a follower of Liverpool FC, the Green Bay Packers, the Atlanta Braves and the Carolina Hurricanes.
